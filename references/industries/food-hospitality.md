# Food and hospitality: what world-class marketing photography looks like

Read the section for the business's industry before writing the brief and shot list (SKILL.md step 3).
House style and Never go into every prompt; Shot concepts seed the shot list; Real only is never generated.

- **Benchmarks** are brands whose photography sets the bar. Brand websites were checked live on
  2026-11-20; Instagram handles were not. "(confirm)" marks a lower-confidence pick. Open the account
  before naming it to a client. Never copy a benchmark's photos: they are cited for style only.
- **Reference photos** are free-licence (Unsplash/Pexels) shots found by text search, not yet viewed.
  Open and judge one before using it as a mood reference; never attach an unviewed image as a
  `style_refs` anchor. Viewed and approved anchors live in `references/anchors/` (see manifest.json).

### Fine-dining and casual restaurants
Also covers: bistro, trattoria, steakhouse, sushi/omakase counter, tasting-menu, gastropub, neighbourhood diner, ethnic restaurants, chef's table, supper club

**Benchmarks**
- Noma (Copenhagen) — https://noma.dk / https://www.instagram.com/noma — dish photographed as an object in the landscape it came from: dark ground, single soft source, no garnish theatre.
- Lilia (Brooklyn) — https://www.instagram.com/lilianewyork — plates, hands and a loud room shot in plain dining-room light; looks lived in, not styled.
- Ottolenghi (London) — https://ottolenghi.co.uk / https://www.instagram.com/ottolenghi — generous overhead and 45-degree table shots with real mess, colour and serving spoons still in the dish.
- Tresind Studio (Dubai) — https://www.instagram.com/tresindstudio — tasting-menu courses shot like objects, with chef hands finishing the plate; Gulf fine-dining at gallery standard.
- Orfali Bros Bistro (Dubai) — https://www.instagram.com/orfalibros — close, saturated, texture-first food shots with brothers working the pass, not posed.
- Odette (Singapore) — https://www.instagram.com/odettesg — pale, art-gallery interior plus single-plate hero shots; restraint is the brand.
- Den (Tokyo) — https://www.instagram.com/den_tokyo — warm domestic wood-and-paper light, chef-host in the frame mid-service.

**House style**
- Light: one soft directional source (window or a single warm practical), deep shadow allowed. Dinner rooms keep candle/pendant warmth in frame instead of correcting it away.
- Lens and distance: 50-85mm equivalent for plates, 24-35mm for rooms and pass. Plate shots from 30-45 degrees or straight down for flat dishes; wide f/2-2.8.
- Colour and grade: warm, slightly desaturated, rich blacks. Real food colour, restrained contrast. Skin tones natural.
- People: chefs and servers working (hands, forearms, backs), guests mid-conversation or mid-bite, looking at food or each other, never at lens. Candid.
- Props, materials, setting: handmade ceramics, linen, worn wood, real glassware with a half-drunk glass; service in progress, crumbs and sauce drag marks are fine.
- Framing: negative space on the plate, cropped partial figures, foreground blur (glass, flame, steam) for depth. Leave room for type.

**Never**
- Perfectly lit white-plate-on-white-table with a sprig of microgreens and no human trace.
- A smiling waiter holding a plate towards camera; a couple clinking glasses at lens.
- HDR dining-room wide shots with every light blown out and empty chairs squared.
- Fake steam/smoke composited on, glossy "plastic" sauces, melting-cheese-pull stock.

**Shot concepts**
- Chef's hands finishing a plate at the pass with tweezers, a second chef's blur behind.
- A server lowering a plate between two diners, only forearm and candle visible.
- Sauce being poured onto a steak from a copper pot, steam backlit by a warm lamp.
- Empty restaurant at 5pm, low sun striping tables set for service.
- Table after the second course: shared plates, half-full glasses, someone reaching in.
- Hands tearing bread over a bowl of olive oil.
- Open kitchen glow seen through the pass from the dining room.
- Sommelier decanting by candle light, face turned to the glass.

**Real only**
- Actual dishes, the real dining room, the real chef and service team, any licensed-venue or signage views, awards/menus.

### Cafe and coffee shop
Also covers: specialty roaster, tea house, juice bar, brunch spot, co-working cafe, matcha bar, kiosk coffee

**Benchmarks**
- Blue Bottle Coffee (Oakland/Tokyo) — https://bluebottlecoffee.com — quiet product-and-hand shots, matte pastel grade, lots of white space.
- % Arabica (Kyoto) — https://arabica.coffee / https://www.instagram.com/arabica.coffee — architectural window framing, one cup, one person silhouette; world-reach minimalism.
- Onibus Coffee (Tokyo) — https://www.instagram.com/onibuscoffee — grainy, human, daylight neighbourhood shots, roasters at work; Asia benchmark.
- The Coffee Collective (Copenhagen) — https://coffeecollective.dk — farm and roastery documentary photography, not cafe cliché.
- Tom&Serg (Dubai) — https://www.instagram.com/tomandserg — brunch-room candid photography, morning light across mismatched tables; Gulf benchmark.
- Nightjar Coffee (Dubai) — https://www.instagram.com/nightjarcoffee — dark, textured roast and pour imagery with a small-batch feel.

**House style**
- Light: side window light, morning, soft; shoot near the glass so light grazes the cup and steam.
- Lens and distance: 35mm for the room, 50-85mm for cups and hands; shoot at counter height for the barista's-eye view.
- Colour and grade: matte, slightly cool shadows, creamy highlights; no teal-orange.
- People: baristas mid-pour, regulars reading or waiting, hands holding cups; faces turned away or in profile.
- Props, materials, setting: ceramic cups, saucers with ring stains, worn counters, real menus on boards, pastry crumbs, scuffed floors.
- Framing: counters as leading lines, partial crops (hand + cup + steam), generous negative space for copy.

**Never**
- A mug of latte art held at lens by a smiling barista.
- Beans scattered artfully on a flat-lay with a laptop and plant.
- A cup floating on a white seamless background.
- Over-sharpened HDR coffee close-ups; fake steam.

**Shot concepts**
- Milk jug tilting at the moment the pour opens the leaf.
- Barista tamping, forearm tattoo and portafilter, grinder blur behind.
- Takeaway cup handed over a counter between two hands, no faces.
- Early light across empty tables just before opening.
- Roaster scooping beans from the cooling tray.
- Two friends in profile at the window, one cup half-drunk.
- Pastry case at the counter with a hand reaching in.
- Pavement-level exterior with a regular walking in, motion blurred.

**Real only**
- The actual cafe interior, signage/menu boards, the real baristas/owners, latte art quality as made in-house, the storefront.

### Bakery and patisserie
Also covers: sourdough micro-bakery, cake studio, chocolatier, donut shop, dessert bar, pastry chef, custom-cake maker

**Benchmarks**
- Tartine (San Francisco) — https://tartinebakery.com / https://www.instagram.com/tartinebakery — flour-dusted hands, crumb close-ups, shot like documentary.
- Lune Croissanterie (Melbourne) — https://www.instagram.com/lunecroissanterie — lamination cross-sections and cutting-board gestures; the pastry as sculpture.
- Bread Ahead (London) — https://breadahead.com — market-stall energy, mess and steam, people queuing.
- Dominique Ansel Bakery (New York) — https://www.instagram.com/dominiqueansel — hero pastry on clean surfaces with a hand entering the frame.
- Cedric Grolet (Paris) — https://www.instagram.com/cedricgrolet — fruit-as-pastry hero shots on dark or stone, one object per frame.
- Sadaharu Aoki (Tokyo/Paris) — https://www.instagram.com/sadaharuaoki — Asia benchmark; pale matcha and glaze precision, vitrine shots.
- Baker & Spice (Dubai) — https://www.instagram.com/bakerandspice — Gulf reference; display-counter and loaf shots in warm daylight (verify current feed style).

**House style**
- Light: soft window light from one side for crumb and glaze highlights; warm bounce off wood. Pre-dawn bake shots keep the oven glow.
- Lens and distance: 50-100mm macro for crumb, 35mm for the bench and counter; 45-degree on tall pastry.
- Colour and grade: warm cream and golden crust; protect highlights on glaze; matte blacks.
- People: baker's forearms and hands, flour on apron, back-of-head at the oven; customers' hands taking a bag.
- Props, materials, setting: floured wood, marble, parchment, steel trays, linen, worn peels; crumbs left on.
- Framing: tight crops on texture; trays in rows as pattern; one bite or cut as the story.

**Never**
- Rows of identical cupcakes with big smiles and a "Happy" banner.
- Overexposed white-on-white cake photos.
- Generic bread-on-checked-cloth with a wheat sprig.
- Plastic-looking sheen or invented pastry shapes; anything that misrepresents what is sold.

**Shot concepts**
- Dough being folded at the bench, hands and dust cloud visible.
- A scoring blade opening a loaf just before the oven.
- Tray of croissants coming out, steam through the oven light.
- Torn croissant showing lamination.
- Glaze poured over a tart, mid-drip.
- Vitrine at golden hour with a hand pointing to a choice.
- A paper bag handed over the counter with a stamp.
- 5am bake: baker silhouetted in oven glow.

**Real only**
- The shop's actual pastries and cakes (custom orders), the vitrine, packaging, the baker's face/hands, anything a customer can order.

### Bar, cocktail bar and nightlife
Also covers: speakeasy, wine bar, craft brewery taproom, rooftop lounge, nightclub, whisky bar, hotel bar, lounge, shisha lounge

**Benchmarks**
- Connaught Bar (London) — https://www.instagram.com/connaughtbar — art-deco room and drinks shot with silverware and single-pool lighting.
- Handshake Speakeasy (Mexico City) — https://www.instagram.com/handshakespeakeasy — drink-as-object photography, dark ground, ice and condensation.
- Jigger & Pony (Singapore) — https://www.instagram.com/jiggerandpony — Asia benchmark; bartender gestures and crisp glassware in low amber light.
- Bar Leone (Hong Kong) — https://www.instagram.com/barleone.hk — packed-room social candid, daylight-to-night shift, Roman-bar warmth.
- Attaboy (New York) — https://www.instagram.com/attaboynyc — grainy, flash-lit, unretouched; the drink and the people at 1am.
- Gulf reference: no verified Gulf bar benchmark; hotel bars in Dubai/Abu Dhabi (e.g. the Ritz-Carlton or Four Seasons bars) run luxe-hotel style. Check Instagram location tags for a local venue; not confirmed here.

**House style**
- Light: low, warm, practical; bar-top candle or back-bar glow as key; flash only for deliberate raw/party looks. Let shadows go black.
- Lens and distance: 35mm for the bartender across the bar, 50-85mm for glass detail; wide open (f/1.4-2).
- Colour and grade: amber, deep blacks, restrained saturation; no teal-orange wash. Noise and grain acceptable, even desirable.
- People: bartender mid-action in profile; guests leaning in, laughing at each other; hands holding glasses; faces half in shadow.
- Props, materials, setting: real ice, steel tools, citrus peel oils misting, bar wood, condensation, bottle labels out of focus.
- Framing: bar-top as foreground, glass cropped by edge, background bokeh lights as depth.

**Never**
- A bartender flashing a smile and pushing a drink toward the lens.
- Neon-teal and magenta "club" fakes; AI-glossy drinks with impossible bubbles.
- Fish-eye empty nightclub with fog machine.
- A bright, evenly lit bar that looks like a daytime cafe.

**Shot concepts**
- Bartender straining a cocktail into a coupe, both hands visible.
- Citrus peel being expressed over a drink, oil mist in the beam.
- Large ice cube hit by amber liquid.
- Two friends clinking out of focus, lit by candle.
- Bar back shelf lit from below, bottles stepping into dark.
- Bartender's stirring spoon spinning in a mixing glass.
- Closing time: stools up, one light on, bartender wiping the bar.
- Rooftop at dusk, drinks on the ledge, skyline softened.

**Real only**
- The venue's actual signature drinks, glassware, interior, signage, the real team; licensed/age-restricted compliance means no faked drinkers.

### Hotel, resort and boutique stay
Also covers: guesthouse, B&B, eco-lodge, desert camp, serviced apartment, villa rental, hostel, glamping, spa retreat

**Benchmarks**
- Aman (Singapore-founded, global) — https://www.aman.com / https://www.instagram.com/amanresorts — empty-room stillness, architecture and landscape, no guests posed.
- Hoshinoya (Japan) — https://hoshinoya.com — Asia benchmark; shot in diffused natural light, ritual details (tea, bath, tatami).
- Six Senses Zighy Bay (Oman) — https://www.sixsenses.com/en/resorts/zighy-bay — Gulf benchmark; mountains-to-sea landscape with guests in small scale.
- Qasr Al Sarab by Anantara (Abu Dhabi) — https://www.anantara.com/en/qasr-al-sarab — desert-dune scale shots at golden hour (verify current style).
- Ace Hotel — https://acehotel.com — lifestyle documentary, shot with locals and creatives.
- Hotel San Cristobal (Baja) — https://www.instagram.com/hotelsancristobal — saturated, art-directed, but people feel casual.

**House style**
- Light: sunrise/sunset side light through windows and sheers; interiors in ambient light, not strobed. Dusk exteriors with lamps on.
- Lens and distance: 24-35mm for rooms (verticals corrected), 50mm vignettes, 85mm for textures and rituals.
- Colour and grade: calm, natural, slightly low contrast, earthy; keep warm interior glow vs. cool dusk window.
- People: guests seen from behind or at small scale in landscape; staff hands (pouring tea, folding towels); no eye contact.
- Props, materials, setting: rumpled linen, local craft, fruit bowl, open book, wet footprints at pool; evidence of stay.
- Framing: doorways and windows as frames, symmetry for architecture, one human trace per frame.

**Never**
- A couple in robes toasting champagne in bed with a smile at the lens.
- Wide-angle HDR rooms with distorted bed edges and every lamp on.
- Pool-with-sunloungers shot at noon, harsh and empty.
- Overly styled breakfast tray with petals; generic infinity-pool stock.

**Shot concepts**
- Morning light across rumpled sheets, window cracked open.
- Guest seen from behind on a terrace looking at the view, coffee in hand.
- Staff laying out tea on a low table.
- Pool at dusk, one lantern and wet footprints.
- Lobby or courtyard in side-light with a person crossing.
- Bathroom detail: stone basin, folded towel, steam.
- Desert or mountain landscape from a villa deck at golden hour.
- Late-night corridor with lamps on and a door slightly ajar.

**Real only**
- The actual rooms, pool, views, staff, signage and any named property, plus availability-relevant details (room type, view, size). Generated imagery must never stand in for the real property.

**Benchmarks**

**House style**

- Lens and distance: 35mm for tables and room, 85mm for details (hands, rings), 24mm for architecture.
- Colour and grade: warm, filmic, soft highlights; keep skin natural. Muted palettes that match the actual styling.
- People: guests in motion (laughing, toasting, passing plates), caterers plating, couple in action not posed; backs and profiles.
- Props, materials, setting: linen, mismatched glass, taper candles, seasonal florals, food in progress, napkins half-used.
- Framing: down the length of a long table, depth through candles, tight details alongside wide establishing shots.

**Never**
- Overexposed white-draped hall with stacked chairs and no guests.
- Staged group lined up grinning; toasts at camera.
- HDR "ballroom" wide shot with every chandelier blown.
- Buffet trays under fluorescent light.

**Shot concepts**
- Long table at dusk, candles lit, guests arriving.
- Caterer plating a course in the pass, tweezers and steam.
- Hands passing a shared platter down the table.
- Cake being cut, candle light on faces from the side.
- Venue empty before an event, sunlight on set tables.
- Bartender pouring welcome drinks on a lawn.
- Detail: place card, napkin fold, menu card (blur text).
- Lights strung over the garden at blue hour.

**Real only**
- The venue and grounds as booked, the real room capacities and layouts, the caterer's real dishes, real past events/guests (with consent), any named location.

### Grocery, deli, specialty food and farm shop
Also covers: butcher, fishmonger, cheesemonger, greengrocer, farmers' market stall, olive oil/spice merchant, wine merchant, gourmet gift hamper maker, health-food store

**Benchmarks**
- Natoora (London) — https://www.natoora.com / https://www.instagram.com/natoora — produce shot as portraits: single variety, soil still on, hands holding.
- Russ & Daughters (New York) — https://www.russanddaughters.com — counter-staff and fish-slicing documentary; heritage in the faces.
- Formaggio Kitchen (Cambridge, MA) — https://www.formaggiokitchen.com — cheese cuts, aging rooms, shop floor with staff handling product.
- Ripe Market (Dubai) — https://www.instagram.com/ripemarket — Gulf reference; producer faces and stalls at weekend market (verify current feed).
- Hunter & Co Deli (Melbourne) — https://www.dezeen.com/2019/02/24/hunter-co-deli-melbourne-interiors-pink-mim-design-australia/ — interior-led deli, colour-coded shelving.
- Asia: no confirmed benchmark found in this pass; consider Tsukiji/Tokyo Ginza specialty food halls or Singapore's Huber's Butchery for a human check.

**House style**
- Light: soft window or market-hall skylight; avoid fluorescent colour casts (balance for the real scene).
- Lens and distance: 35-50mm in-shop, 85mm for product close-ups; shoot at 30-45 degrees onto counters.
- Colour and grade: true-to-life produce colour, slightly warm shadows; no heavy saturation.
- People: staff cutting, weighing, wrapping, offering a taste; shoppers choosing, hands on product; producers at the stall.
- Props, materials, setting: butcher paper, brown bags, scales, crates, chalk boards, scuffed tiles; product handled, not pristine.
- Framing: counters as foreground, repeating rows (jars, cheeses), cropped hands, close-ups of cut surfaces.

**Never**
- A staff member smiling at lens holding a basket of vegetables.
- Perfect pyramid fruit on a white background with leaf props.
- Supermarket flyer HDR with neon-saturated produce.
- Generic "farmers market" crate with an invented logo; AI produce that is too symmetrical.

**Shot concepts**
- Cheesemonger cutting a wedge with a wire.
- Butcher wrapping an order in paper, string in teeth.
- Hands selecting tomatoes at the stall.
- Counter at opening time, labelled trays being set.
- Producer unloading crates at dawn.
- Customer tasting from a toothpick, laughing off-camera.
- Shelves of jars lit by window light, one gap where a jar was taken.
- Hamper being packed, hands tying twine.

**Real only**
- The actual products, labels, producers and staff, counters and shelf contents (to avoid misleading shoppers), certifications (organic, halal), price boards.

### Quick-service, food truck and delivery kitchen
Also covers: burger/fried chicken counter, shawarma/falafel stand, street-food stall, ramen counter, pizza by the slice, ghost kitchen, juice/smoothie bar, sandwich shop, hawker stall

**Benchmarks**
- Shake Shack (New York) — https://shakeshack.com / https://www.instagram.com/shakeshack — close, bright, honest burger product shots with hands and trays.
- Sweetgreen (US) — https://www.sweetgreen.com — colour-forward bowls in daylight, assembly-line hands.
- Dishoom (London) — https://www.dishoom.com / https://www.instagram.com/dishoom — Bombay cafe storytelling: steam, tea glasses, packed tables, warm and candid.
- Salt (Dubai) — https://www.instagram.com/salt — Gulf benchmark; beach-and-truck burger scene, people-led and casual (verify current feed).
- Afuri (Tokyo) — https://afuri.com — Asia benchmark; yuzu-shio ramen, steam and bowl detail, minimalist.
- Honest Burgers (UK) — https://www.honestburgers.co.uk — real burgers, mess intact, shot on wood and paper.

**House style**
- Light: natural or daylight on the counter; night shots use the stall's own bulbs and neon; avoid ring-flash.
- Lens and distance: 35mm for the stall and queue, 50-85mm for the food in hand; shoot at street level.
- Colour and grade: punchy but real; warm highlights, no sodium orange cast; slight grain acceptable.
- People: cook at the grill, hands passing a tray, customers mid-bite or queueing; faces turned.
- Props, materials, setting: paper wrappers, steel trays, griddle smoke, branded-but-blurred packaging, real menu boards.
- Framing: counter-height, tight on hands and product, wide for queue and neon; leave room for type.

**Never**
- Perfectly assembled burger with fake glossy sauce and sesame seeds placed with tweezers.
- A cheerful cashier holding a bag towards lens.
- Delivery-bag-on-white-background with a scooter icon.
- Fake smoke, neon-teal/magenta night-market grade, AI food geometry.

**Shot concepts**
- Cook flipping on the griddle, smoke and steam backlit.
- Paper-wrapped burger cut open, sauce dripping.
- Hand passing a tray through the truck window.
- Queue at night under string lights, backs to camera.
- Bag being sealed with a sticker on the delivery line.
- Close on noodles lifted from broth.
- Truck at golden hour with a street scene behind.
- Customer's hands tearing a wrap on a kerb.

**Real only**
- The actual menu items, trucks/storefronts, packaging, real food as served, staff and queues, hygiene ratings and licences.
