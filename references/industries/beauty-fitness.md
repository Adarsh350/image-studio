# Beauty, wellness and fitness: what world-class marketing photography looks like

Read the section for the business's industry before writing the brief and shot list (SKILL.md step 3).
House style and Never go into every prompt; Shot concepts seed the shot list; Real only is never generated.

- **Benchmarks** are brands whose photography sets the bar. Brand websites were checked live on
  2026-11-20; Instagram handles were not. "(confirm)" marks a lower-confidence pick. Open the account
  before naming it to a client. Never copy a benchmark's photos: they are cited for style only.
- **Reference photos** are free-licence (Unsplash/Pexels) shots found by text search, not yet viewed.
  Open and judge one before using it as a mood reference; never attach an unviewed image as a
  `style_refs` anchor. Viewed and approved anchors live in `references/anchors/` (see manifest.json).

### Hair salon
Also covers: colour studio, blow-dry bar, bridal hair, hair extensions studio, Afro/textured hair specialist, hairdresser in a hotel

**Benchmarks**
- Sally Hershberger (New York/LA) — https://www.sallyhershberger.com — editorial hair photography: movement and texture, not a posed "finished style" on a smiling model.
- Hershesons (London) — https://www.hershesons.com — real stylists and chairs in frame, daylight from big windows, hair shot mid-movement.
- Bastide-style neighbourhood studios such as Cutler Salon (New York) — https://www.instagram.com/cutlersalon — colour results shot in natural light on real clients with unretouched texture.
- Sam McKnight (London) — https://www.instagram.com/sammcknight — fashion-grade hair stills; product and process shown as craft.
- Kérastase salon campaigns (Paris) — https://www.kerastase.com — sculptural close crops of hair in motion against flat colour.
- Gulf: Ruffles/ The Gents Place-tier Dubai salons such as Hairroin (Dubai) — https://www.instagram.com/hairroin (confirm) — colour transformations on real clients, window light.
- Asia: Garden Tokyo (Tokyo) — https://www.instagram.com/garden_tokyo (confirm) — soft grey-beige Japanese salon portraits, real texture, no gloss.

**House style**
- Light: soft window light from the side or behind, no ring light and no flash; overcast daylight for colour accuracy.
- Lens and distance: 50-85mm, wide-open aperture; close on hands and hair, or three-quarter on stylist and client.
- Colour and grade: neutral and slightly desaturated; true hair colour matters; film-like grain acceptable; never teal-orange.
- People: real stylist mid-task (comb, scissors, foils), client relaxed or looking down or away, rarely at lens.
- Props, materials, setting: capes, clips, mirrors, real products on the trolley, wet-room towels; imperfect and in use.
- Framing: hair back-of-head and profile, mirror reflections, hands plus hair as the subject; plenty of negative space.

**Never**
- Model with perfect blow-out grinning at the camera over a white backdrop.
- Over-smoothed, glossy-filtered hair and plastic skin.
- Floating scissors and comb "tool flat-lay" on pure white.
- Wig-on-mannequin heads as the hero image.
- Before/after with different lighting between the two frames.

**Shot concepts**
- Stylist sectioning damp hair with clips, hands and comb only.
- Foils being applied during a highlight, bowl and brush in frame.
- Scissor cut on the ends, hair falling, shallow focus.
- Back-of-head reveal in the mirror, client reaching up to feel the cut.
- Round-brush blow-dry in side window light, hair lifting.
- Shampoo basin moment, hands in lather, towel at the neck.
- Colour shelf and mixing bowl, tint swirls on the brush.
- Empty chair and mirror at opening time with morning light across the floor.

**Real only**
- The actual stylist and the salon's own interior.
- Colour and cut results on real clients (before/after, hair length, tone).
- Team portraits and any client likeness.

### Barbershop
Also covers: men's grooming lounge, beard-trim and hot-towel shave bar, kids' barber, traditional Turkish/Arabic barber

**Benchmarks**
- Fellow Barber (New York/San Francisco) — https://www.fellowbarber.com — warm interior, real barbers and clientele, magazine-quality craft shots.
- Blind Barber (New York) — https://www.blindbarber.com — bar-meets-barbershop atmosphere, dim practical lighting, candid.
- Murdock London (London) — https://www.murdocklondon.com — heritage tools and leather, close-up craft.
- Schorem (Rotterdam) — https://www.schorem.com — design-forward barbershop identity with serious portraits.
- Chaps & Co (Dubai/London) — https://www.instagram.com/chapsandco (confirm) — lived-in modern shop, barbers at work.
- Gulf: The Gents Place (Dubai) — https://www.instagram.com/thegentsplace (confirm) — upscale grooming lounge.
- Asia: Tokyo and Seoul independent barbers such as Hiro Ginza (Tokyo) — https://www.instagram.com/hiroginza (confirm) — immaculate craft close-ups.

**House style**
- Light: warm tungsten pendants plus window spill; deliberate pools of light on the chair; practical lighting only.
- Lens and distance: 35mm for the shop, 85mm for the fade; very tight crops on clipper-to-skin.
- Colour and grade: warm shadows, deep blacks, restrained saturation; black and white works for portraits.
- People: barber focused on the work, client eyes closed or looking aside; no smiling at lens.
- Props, materials, setting: leather chair, straight razor, hot towel, brush, tiled floor, wood and brick; real mess of hair.
- Framing: hands and tool close-ups, profile of the fade, reflected barber in the mirror, symmetrical shop interiors.

**Never**
- Studio-white background with a model holding clippers.
- Tattooed-hipster cliché props that do not exist in the shop.
- Fake hot-towel steam added in post.
- Smiling duo "handshake after the cut" shot.
- HDR-crunched fade lines.

**Shot concepts**
- Clipper-guard fade, skin-level close-up, hair on the cape.
- Straight-razor neckline, barber's off-hand holding the ear.
- Hot towel wrap, steam rising in lamp light.
- Beard shaping with scissors and comb.
- Barber stropping a razor.
- Chair-row view down the shop at golden hour.
- Client checking the back with the hand mirror.
- Sweeping up at close, clippers lined up on the bench.

**Real only**
- The actual shop, chairs and barbers.
- Real client fades and beards (for style and lengths).
- Team portraits.

### Nail, lash and brow studio
Also covers: beauty bar, waxing and threading studio, permanent makeup, mobile manicurist, lash lift, microblading

**Benchmarks**
- Nail Hack / Hang Nails-style editorial: Mei Kawajiri (Tokyo/London) — https://www.instagram.com/meinailartist — nail art as fine-art macro photography.
- Dazzle Dry-type brands are poor benchmarks; instead Lenox Nails (Los Angeles) — https://www.instagram.com/lenox.nails (confirm) — documentary detail on real hands.
- Sophy Robson (London) — https://www.instagram.com/sophyrobson — nail still lifes with soft shadow and real surface.
- Tenoverten (New York) — https://www.tenoverten.com — design-led salon, light-filled interiors, hands at work.
- Brow: Sania Vucetaj (New York) — https://www.instagram.com/sania_vucetaj (confirm) — restrained before/during shots in natural light.
- Gulf: Nails of Dubai-tier studios such as Nail Social/Colour Me Beautiful (Dubai) — confirm handle; look for window-lit manicure tables.
- Asia: Japanese gel-nail salons, e.g. Aoyama nail studios — https://www.instagram.com/explore/tags/ネイルサロン (tag page only; confirm a specific studio).

**House style**
- Light: soft diffused window light, a single warm lamp over the table; no flash glare on gel.
- Lens and distance: 100mm macro or 85mm; hands fill the frame.
- Colour and grade: true polish colour; clean neutral whites without blown highlights; tonal harmony with the surface.
- People: the technician's hands plus the client's hand; client face rarely visible; lash close-ups with eyes gently closed.
- Props, materials, setting: linen towels, tray of polishes, UV lamp, tweezers on a pad; real salon bench.
- Framing: macro on the nail bed, top-down hand on towel, eyelid with lash isolation.

**Never**
- Fake hand with fake nails against pure white seamless.
- Over-smoothed skin and cuticles retouched away completely.
- Rainbow nail-polish bottle pyramids in a studio.
- Wide-open eyes staring into the lens in a lash shot.
- Glitter-effect and lens-flare overlays.

**Shot concepts**
- Brush stroking polish at the cuticle, macro.
- Gel cure under the UV lamp, glow on fingers.
- Tweezers isolating a single lash and fanning extension.
- Brow shaping with thread, tight on the arch.
- Client's hand resting on a towel during a manicure.
- Row of polish bottles on the shelf, one pulled for use.
- Technician cleaning a cuticle with a pusher.
- Finished set on a plain linen with a jewellery piece.

**Real only**
- The studio's actual nail art and lash sets (client results).
- The technician's hands and station.
- Before/after results and client likeness.

### Skincare, cosmetics and fragrance product brands
Also covers: haircare brand, body-care and candle brand, nail-polish brand, DTC beauty startup, perfume house, wellness supplement brand

**Benchmarks**
- Aesop (Melbourne) — https://www.aesop.com — amber bottles in architectural settings, warm shadow, material-led.
- Le Labo (New York) — https://www.lelabofragrances.com — raw, flash-free laboratory look.
- Glossier (New York) — https://www.glossier.com — real skin, bathroom-mirror, casual light.
- Byredo (Stockholm) — https://www.byredo.com — editorial still life, stark and tactile.
- Augustinus Bader / Tata Harper — https://www.tataharper.com — farm-to-face natural setting.
- Gulf: Amouage (Muscat) — https://www.amouage.com — dark luxury still life, deep red and gold, fragrance as object.
- Asia: Shiseido (Tokyo) — https://www.shiseido.com — soft daylight precision; also Sulwhasoo (Seoul) — https://www.sulwhasoo.com — traditional materials in modern light.

**House style**
- Light: single hard window or flash-with-flag shadow; long, directional shadows; no flat softbox-on-white.
- Lens and distance: 100mm macro for texture, 50mm for context.
- Colour and grade: earthy or tonal palettes keyed to the product; skin texture visible; no teal-orange.
- People: hands applying product, skin close-ups with pores, never grinning model.
- Props, materials, setting: stone, linen, plaster, water, bathroom shelf, real shadows, leaf shadows.
- Framing: product off-centre with negative space, texture smears, hand holding product.

**Never**
- Product floating on pure white with a reflection.
- Rose-petal-and-citrus clutter around every bottle.
- Smiling model holding a tube beside her cheek.
- Fake water splashes and CGI-glow orbs.
- Readable competitor brand labels as the hero.

**Shot concepts**
- Dropper releasing serum in side light.
- Cream swatch smeared across a stone.
- Hand pumping a bottle of body lotion over a sink.

- Asia: Horiyoshi III (Yokohama) — https://www.horiyoshi3.com (confirm) — traditional irezumi documented with archival reverence.
- Seoul/Tokyo fine-line studios such as Mini Tattoo (confirm).

**House style**
- Light: single hard lamp on the work plus ambient dark; healed-skin shots in window light.
- Lens and distance: 85-100mm macro on skin and needle; 35mm for the studio.
- Colour and grade: deep shadow, saturated ink, honest skin tone with redness; B&W for the process.
- People: artist bent over the work, face half-hidden; client relaxed or looking away; masks and gloves.
- Props, materials, setting: machine, grip tape, stencil, ink caps, wall flash, bench lamp, wrap.
- Framing: needle-to-skin tight crop, flash wall, finished piece in profile.

**Never**
- Tattoo clichés: skull-and-rose props in a staged white set.
- Tattooed model shirtless with a cigarette at lens.
- Stock skull-head studio signage generated by AI.
- Neon-lit fake studio with no real artist.
- Retouched ink that looks like decals.

**Shot concepts**
- Needle contacting skin, ink bead on the line.
- Stencil transfer being pressed on an arm.
- Artist mixing ink caps at the bench.
- Healed tattoo in window light, a week later.
- Flash sheet pinned on the wall.
- Artist wiping the skin mid-session.
- Machine and tubes laid out under the lamp.
- Client checking the stencil in the mirror.

**Real only**
- The artist's actual tattoos and portfolio.
