# Health and care: what world-class marketing photography looks like

Read the section for the business's industry before writing the brief and shot list (SKILL.md step 3).
House style and Never go into every prompt; Shot concepts seed the shot list; Real only is never generated.

- **Benchmarks** are brands whose photography sets the bar. Brand websites were checked live on
  2026-11-20; Instagram handles were not. "(confirm)" marks a lower-confidence pick. Open the account
  before naming it to a client. Never copy a benchmark's photos: they are cited for style only.
- **Reference photos** are free-licence (Unsplash/Pexels) shots found by text search, not yet viewed.
  Open and judge one before using it as a mood reference; never attach an unviewed image as a
  `style_refs` anchor. Viewed and approved anchors live in `references/anchors/` (see manifest.json).

### Dental clinic and orthodontics
Also covers: dental hygienist, cosmetic dentistry, implant centre, paediatric dentist, oral surgeon

**Benchmarks**
- Quip (New York, US) — https://www.getquip.com — Product-and-ritual photography on clean saturated colour; teeth are never the subject, the routine is.
- Candid (US) — https://www.candidco.com — Real-feeling bathroom and hand-held-device moments, lifestyle grade, no clinical blue.
- Smile Design Dental Clinic (Dubai, UAE) — https://www.instagram.com/smiledesigndubai — Gulf premium positioning; interior and equipment shots rather than veneer smiles.
- Dentons / Bupa Dental style independents such as Bow Lane Dental (London, UK) — https://www.instagram.com/bowlanedental — Architectural interiors, warm daylight, team shot in the actual surgery.
- Sunstar / Ginza Dental clinics (Tokyo, Japan) — https://www.instagram.com/explore/tags/ginzadental — Japanese clinics shoot spare, hotel-like treatment rooms and instruments laid out like jewellery.
- Hello Smile (Singapore) — https://www.instagram.com/hellosmile.sg — Soft-toned candid patient and dentist moments, restrained palette.

**House style**
- Light: large window light or soft overhead diffused; no flash; skin stays warm, instruments stay cool.
- Lens and distance: 35-50mm for rooms; 85-100mm macro for instruments and gloved hands. Shoot from patient eye height.
- Colour and grade: muted warm whites, one accent (clay, sage, brand colour); gentle grade, no cyan cast.
- People: dentist or hygienist mid-task looking at the work, patient relaxed, eyes closed or looking at the practitioner. Never eye contact plus grin.
- Props, materials, setting: real chair, real instruments, wood and plaster surfaces, plants, a reception that looks like a boutique hotel.
- Framing: asymmetric, negative space for copy, tight crops of hands and tools.

**Never**
- Perfect-teeth grin close-up with white seamless background.
- Blue-tinted clinical rooms with floating tooth icons or 3D molars.
- Dentist pointing at an X-ray on a monitor.
- Thumbs-up patient in a chair, headrest bib, mask pulled down to grin.
- Over-retouched veneers that look generated.

**Shot concepts**
- Gloved hand lifting a mirror and probe, window light behind.
- Hygienist adjusting the overhead lamp, patient reclined and calm.
- Instruments laid out on a tray at the start of the day.
- Child in the chair holding a stuffed toy, dentist at eye level.
- Reception desk with the receptionist handing over a card, plants behind.
- Dentist and nurse reviewing a scan at the end of a long day, tired and real.
- Treatment room with morning light, chair empty.
- Aligner case and a glass of water on a bathroom shelf, patient's hand.

**Real only**
- Patient smiles and before/after results.
- The actual team and the actual treatment room.
- Any shot implying a specific patient outcome.

### GP and medical clinic or hospital
Also covers: urgent care, specialist consultant practice, diagnostics lab, polyclinic, walk-in clinic

**Benchmarks**
- One Medical (US) — https://www.onemedical.com — Calm, lifestyle-grade interiors and plain-clothed clinicians.
- Parsley Health (US) — https://www.parsleyhealth.com — Editorial food, light and portrait photography that reads like a wellness magazine.
- Hims and Hers (US) — https://www.forhims.com — Bold colour product and portrait work; the health context is implied.
- Mayo Clinic brand imagery (Rochester, US) — https://www.mayoclinic.org — Documentary-style real staff and patients, restrained, no stock look.
- Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi (UAE) — https://www.clevelandclinicabudhabi.ae — Gulf benchmark for architectural interiors and calm patient journeys.
- Raffles Medical (Singapore) — https://www.rafflesmedical.com — Asia benchmark; polished but staff-led.
- Kry / Livi (Sweden) — https://www.kry.se — Telehealth with candid home-setting patient photography.

**House style**
- Light: window daylight, soft, warm; hospital corridors with real ambient light.
- Lens and distance: 35mm environmental for rooms and corridors; 85mm for staff portraits.
- Colour and grade: neutral, slightly warm; avoid fluorescent green and cyan cast.
- People: real clinicians doing real work, looking at patient or notes; patients from varied ages in ordinary clothes.
- Props, materials, setting: stethoscope on a desk, handwritten chart, plant and timber in consult room; no cheesy lab coats against white.
- Framing: observational, over-the-shoulder, doorway views.

**Never**
- Doctor with arms crossed and stethoscope smiling at camera.
- Handshake across a desk.
- Doctor pointing at a tablet or hologram.
- Floating medical-icon overlays.
- Teal-blue grade on everything.

**Shot concepts**
- GP typing notes while the patient talks, shot over the shoulder.
- Nurse taking a blood-pressure reading on an older patient.
- Reception desk at opening, first patient arriving.
- Corridor with morning sun and an empty trolley.
- Pharmacist-style hand-off of a prescription, hands only.
- Child on the examination bed with a parent, doctor kneeling.
- Staff huddle at shift change, candid.
- Waiting room with a patient reading, plant and wood.

**Real only**
- Actual clinicians, patients and facility exteriors.
- Any outcome or hospital-equipment claim.

- Ayla Clinic (Singapore) — aylaclinic.com (dead link 2026-11-20: find the current site) — Understated clinic photography with warm earth tones.

**House style**
- Light: soft window light or diffused strip light, skin tone first; no beauty-dish glam.
- Lens and distance: 85-100mm for skin and hands; 35mm for rooms.
- Colour and grade: skin-true, warm neutrals, low saturation; visible pores are a feature.
- People: practitioner's gloved hands working, client's face partly turned or eyes closed.
- Props, materials, setting: linen, marble, ceramic, a lit lamp, a real treatment bed.
- Framing: cropped, intimate, mostly half-faces and hands.

**Never**
- Airbrushed plastic skin on a white background.
- Woman touching her cheek, looking at camera, with a grin.
- Syringe held to a camera lens like a menacing prop.
- Before/after splits on a generated face.
- Cucumber on eyes and bamboo spa tropes.

**Shot concepts**
- Practitioner's gloved hand mapping injection points with a pen.
- Client in the chair, eyes closed, light on the cheekbone.
- Close-up of a microneedling or laser pass, skin texture visible.
- Consultation with a hand mirror at a window.
- Tray of vials and instruments, soft side light.
- Client leaving the clinic, bare-faced, looking away.
- Practitioner washing hands before a treatment.
- Product shelf in the treatment room, backlit.

**Real only**
- Any result, before/after or skin outcome.
- Real clients and the real practitioner.

- Whoop recovery imagery (US) — https://www.whoop.com — Moody, sweaty documentary tone.

- Lens and distance: 35mm environmental; 85mm for hands on joints.
- Colour and grade: warm skin, desaturated greys, matte.
- People: therapist mid-technique looking at the joint; patient focused or exhaling, not smiling.
- Props, materials, setting: treatment table with paper roll, resistance bands, foam roller, stairs and parallel bars.
- Framing: close hands-on crops and wide movement shots with space for copy.

**Never**
- Smiling patient doing a thumbs-up on a gym ball.
- Skeleton model with a red pain spot.

**Shot concepts**
- Hands working a tight shoulder, therapist watching the tissue.
- Shockwave or ultrasound probe on an elbow.
- Patient doing a single-leg balance, therapist spotting.
- Sports player tying a shoe in the waiting area, ankle taped.
- Stretching in the corridor between sessions.
- Therapist writing the exercise plan by hand.
- Back-of-patient walking the gait lane, therapist behind.
- Clinic hands drying after a session.

**Real only**

- The therapist and the clinic.

**House style**
- Light: daylight, soft, low angles; no direct flash on animals.

- People: vet crouched at animal level, owner holding paw or stroking, eyes on the animal.
- Props, materials, setting: stainless exam table, folded towel, treat jar, tags, a clinic dog asleep in reception.
- Framing: eye-level with the animal, tight on interaction.

- Vet holding a puppy and grinning at the camera.

- Stock cat in a cone looking sad with a stethoscope placed for show.

- Puppy first-vaccine day, nurse crouched with treats.
- Vet handwriting notes beside a sleeping animal.
- Owner and dog in the waiting room.
- Hands wrapping a bandage on a paw.
- Clinic cat in the window.

**Real only**

- Procedure and outcome photos.
- The clinic team.

- Headspace (US) — https://www.headspace.com — Illustrated and photo hybrid with human warmth, never clinical.

**House style**
- Light: soft window daylight, lamp glow in the evening; no spotlights.
- Lens and distance: 35-50mm environmental; shallow depth of field for hands and objects.
- Colour and grade: warm neutral, low contrast, earth tones, no cold blue.
- People: faces often turned away or in profile; therapist listening with a notebook; hands more than faces.
- Props, materials, setting: two chairs at an angle, tissue box, plant, throw blanket, books, tea; wood and linen.
- Framing: quiet, asymmetric, empty chair as a subject.

**Never**
- Person with head in hands against a grey sky.
- Silhouette on a cliff at sunset.
- Therapist and patient both smiling at the camera.
- Brain graphic overlaid on a head.
- Stock couple holding hands on a couch for no reason.

**Shot concepts**
- Therapist's notebook and pen mid-session, patient's hands visible.
- Two armchairs angled toward a window.
- Client arriving at the door of the practice, back to camera.
- Tissue box and glass of water on a side table.
- Therapist in a waiting-room doorway, calling a name.
- Journaling at a kitchen table, morning.
- Walk-and-talk session in a park.
- Bookshelf and lamp, therapist's desk at dusk.

**Real only**
- Real clients and sessions (never imply a specific client).
- The therapist and the actual room.
