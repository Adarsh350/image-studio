# Industry index: find the reference section for any business

Every run reads one industry section before writing the brief (SKILL.md step 3). Find the
business below: the bold name is the section, then the businesses it also covers. Open
`<file>.md` and read that `### <section>`.

## How to use a section

- **House style:** write it into the brief and use its light, lens and grade lines in every
  generate prompt. Where the site's own good photos disagree with it, the site wins: the set must
  sit beside them without a seam.
- **Never:** add to the reject gate for this run (craft.md section 4, Brand misses).
- **Shot concepts:** seed the shot list. Rewrite each one for this business's place and customers.
- **Real only:** these slots are never `generate`. With no harvested photo they become another
  concept, and the reply lists them as shots the owner should take.
- **Benchmarks:** how the best in the industry shoot. Use them to judge the bar, never as images
  to copy. **Reference photos:** free-licence mood references, not yet viewed (see each file's
  header).

## No exact match

1. Pick the nearest section by what the customer sees: a car-wrap shop is "Auto repair, tyres and
   detailing", a dog groomer is "Veterinary clinic", a pottery class is "Education, tutoring and
   schools" for people and "Furniture and homeware store" for the product.
2. A business that spans two sections (a cafe that roasts and sells beans) reads both, using one for
   the space and the other for the product.
3. Nothing fits (a niche like a falconry centre or a crematorium): before the shot list, spend
   about five minutes on a live pass. Run WebSearch for "best <industry> brand photography" and
   "<industry> Instagram", and open three to five benchmark sites. Then write a section in the same
   shape (Benchmarks, House style, Never, 8 Shot concepts, Real only) into `$W/industry.md` and use it.
   If it would serve again, add it to the nearest file here.

## Style anchors

`../anchors/manifest.json` lists every anchor with its `look`, the `industries` (as
`file#Section`) it was picked for, its source, creator and licence, and why it clears the bar. Pick
by look first (craft.md, "Style anchors"): an anchor from another industry with the right light and
grade beats a same-industry anchor with the wrong one. Filter with:

```bash
python -I -c "import json,sys; [print(a['file'], a['look'], a['why']) for a in json.load(open(sys.argv[1], encoding='utf-8')) if sys.argv[2] in a['look'] + ' '.join(a['industries'])]" <skill-dir>/references/anchors/manifest.json "Barbershop"
```
